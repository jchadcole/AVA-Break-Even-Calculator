The whole idea, with two agents

A tiny contact center with round numbers, worked through by hand. The calculator gives the same answers when it is given the same inputs.

1Meet the team

Two agents. They do one job: answer calls. Each is paid for 100 hours a month and costs $6,000 a month.

Not every paid hour is call time. 20 hours go to breaks, training and meetings (20% shrinkage). Of the 80 hours left, agents spend 75% actually on calls (occupancy). The rest is waiting for the next call.

Breaks, training, meetingsWaiting for a callOn calls
100 paid hr × (100% − 20% shrinkage) × 75% occupancy = 60 hr of calls per agent

2The calls

Each call takes 6 minutes, so an agent handles 10 calls in an hour of call time. Sixty hours of call time is 600 calls per agent, and 1,200 calls a month for the two of them. Each icon below is 100 calls.

60 hr × 60 min ÷ 6 min = 600 calls per agent · 2 agents = 1,200 calls a month
$6,000 ÷ 60 hr = $100 per hour of calls · $6,000 ÷ 600 calls = $10 of agent time per call

3AVA answers half the calls

Every call now goes to AVA first. AVA fully resolves half of them, 600 calls. The other 600 are passed to an agent.

600 calls × 6 minutes = 60 hours of call work that no agent has to do. That is exactly one agent's call time, so one agent is freed.

AVA is not free. It charges $2 for every call it answers, including the ones it passes on to an agent: 1,200 calls × $2 = $2,400 a month.

600 resolved calls × 6 min ÷ 60 = 60 hours automated · 60 hr ÷ 60 hr per agent = 1 agent freed
AVA bill = 1,200 calls × $2 = $2,400

4The monthly bill

$3,600saved every month (36 agent hours)

1 of 2agents freed

Saved: $12,000 − $8,400 = $3,600 a month × 12 = $43,200 a year · In hours: $3,600 ÷ $100 = 36 agent hours

5Where it breaks even

Each call AVA resolves saves $10 of agent time. AVA's bill is $2,400 whatever share it resolves, because it charges for every call it answers. So AVA pays for itself once it resolves 240 calls, which is 20% of calls.

$2,400 AVA bill ÷ $10 saved per call = 240 calls · 240 ÷ 1,200 = 20% break-even

+Why only call time counts

Suppose these agents also answered email for part of the day. AVA would still take only the 60 hours of call work. The email work would stay, so the agent freed is worth 60 hours, not a full day's work. That is why the calculator counts only the hours AVA takes over, and why "working time on these calls" doesn't increase the savings.
